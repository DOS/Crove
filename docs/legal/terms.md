1. **Parties and scope.**
   - These terms bind [Operator name], [address], tax code [code] (the **Operator**) and whoever creates an account or accepts an invitation to an organization on Crove at [site] (the **Customer**).
   - Crove receives invoices and documents, proposes journal entries for a person with the right to approve them, and exports to the Customer's accounting software.
   - The Crove source code is AGPL. These terms cover the Operator's service, not the code licence.
   - An accounting firm that uses Crove for its client companies is the Customer. It is responsible for having the client companies' consent and legal basis.
2. **Accounts and members.**
   - Give accurate information and keep passwords secret.
   - Turn on two-factor authentication when Crove asks. Approving documents requires it.
   - Owners and admins decide who is a member and what each member may do. Members' actions are the Customer's.
   - Report suspected misuse to [support channel] at once.
3. **Customer data.**
   - Documents, invoices, ledgers and everything the Customer puts in Crove belong to the Customer. The Operator processes them only to provide the service, under the [Privacy Notice](privacy.md) and the Customer's instructions.
   - AI reads PDFs and photos. XML e-invoices are read directly, without AI.
   - Every proposal is checked by rules and approved by a person before it is posted. The Customer is responsible for the figures it approved.
   - The Customer can export all of an organization's data at any time.
   - The Customer keeps accounting records for the period the Law on Accounting requires. Crove does not take over that duty.
4. **Acceptable use.** No data the Customer may not process. No forged documents or tax evasion. No attempts to reach other organizations' data, attack or overload the system, or send spam.
5. **Fees.**
   - Fees, billing period and trial follow [price list or pilot agreement].
   - After [n] days of overdue payment following a reminder, the Operator may suspend writes. Reading and exporting stay available.
6. **Service level.** Daily backups, at most 24 hours of data loss and restore within 4 hours after a major incident. These are targets, not compensated commitments, unless a separate contract says otherwise. Maintenance may interrupt the service, announced in advance when possible.
7. **Liability.**
   - Crove assists bookkeeping. It is not an accounting or tax advisory service, and the Customer's people check and answer for the figures on returns and reports.
   - To the extent the law allows, total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before the event. [Lawyer to confirm, question 7.]
   - The Operator is not liable for wrong approvals, wrong information supplied by the Customer, or force majeure.
8. **Termination.**
   - The Customer may stop and close an organization at any time.
   - The Operator may terminate for a serious breach of section 4, with [n] days' notice except in an emergency.
   - After termination the Customer has [30] days to export. The organization's data is then deleted, and backups follow their lifecycle of at most [n] days. Audit records are kept only where the law requires.
9. **Changes.** At least [30] days' notice for changes against the Customer. Continued use after the effective date means acceptance.
10. **Law.** Vietnamese law. Disputes are negotiated first, then go to the competent court in [place].
