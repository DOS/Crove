1. **Definitions.** "You" is the individual who submits a Contribution. "Contribution" is any original work of authorship, including modifications or additions to existing work, that you intentionally submit to the Project Owner for inclusion in the Project, by any means (pull request, patch, issue, email). If any entity brings patent litigation alleging that your Contribution or the Project constitutes patent infringement, the patent licenses granted to that entity under this agreement end on the date the litigation is filed.

4. **Your authority.** You represent that you are legally entitled to grant these licenses. If your employer has rights to intellectual property you create, you represent that you have received permission to make the Contribution on behalf of your employer, or that your employer has waived those rights for your Contributions, or that your employer has signed the Corporate CLA in Part 2.

5. **Original work.** You represent that each Contribution is your original creation. You will identify in the submission any third-party material you include, with its source and license terms, and mark it conspicuously as not your own.

6. **No obligation; no warranty.** You are not expected to provide support. 8. **Moral rights.** To the extent permitted by law, you waive or agree not to assert moral rights against the Project Owner and its licensees in the Contribution.

## Part 2: Corporate Contributor License Agreement

This part applies when a company or other legal entity ("Corporation") authorizes its employees or contractors to contribute.

1. The Corporation accepts the terms of Part 1, with "You" meaning the Corporation, and grants the same copyright and patent licenses (including the right to sublicense and relicense) for all Contributions made by its Designated Employees on its behalf, or that are owned by it.
2. The Corporation represents that it has the legal authority to enter into this agreement and that the person signing is authorized to sign for it.
3. The Corporation designates the individuals listed in a schedule (name, GitHub username, email) as "Designated Employees" authorized to submit Contributions on its behalf. It may update the schedule by written notice, and the Project Owner may rely on the schedule as current.
4. Each Designated Employee is also expected to accept Part 1 for personal Contributions, but Contributions made on behalf of the Corporation are covered by this Part.
5. The Corporation will notify the Project Owner of any change that makes a representation here inaccurate, and may end the authorization for future Contributions by written notice; Contributions already submitted stay licensed.
